# Demo Script

## 2-Minute Demo Script

1. Open the app and frame it:
   - "This is a research-only AI quant workstation for analyzing assets and paper-trade decision support."
2. Show Executive Dashboard:
   - Headline verdict, top risks, and agent consensus.
3. Jump to Market Overview:
   - Snapshot, regime, and screener.
4. Jump to Strategy Lab:
   - Strategy comparison and walk-forward testing.
5. Jump to AI Research:
   - Workflow orchestrator, sub-agent review board, and meta decision.
6. Close with safety:
   - "No live trading, no broker APIs, no auto execution."

## 5-Minute Demo Script

1. Problem and thesis (30s):
   - Retail research is fragmented and often overconfident.
   - This app centralizes research logic with explicit safety boundaries.
2. Market context flow (1m):
   - Market snapshot, risk metrics, macro + sector context.
   - Cross-asset and opportunity ranking outputs.
3. Strategy validation flow (1m):
   - Backtest + strategy lab + walk-forward fit.
   - Explain that this is for hypothesis testing, not prediction certainty.
4. Decision-support stack (1.5m):
   - Conviction, execution readiness, position sizing, entry/exit.
   - Meta decision and sub-agent board as final quality filters.
5. Learning/logging flow (45s):
   - Prediction and research run logs, health check, DB status.
6. Safety and roadmap (45s):
   - Research-only rules and future planned improvements.

## What to Click Through

1. Executive Dashboard
2. Market Overview tab
3. Strategy Lab tab
4. AI Research tab
5. Learning & Logs tab

## How to Explain the App

- "It combines quant signals, risk controls, and AI-readable summaries."
- "It is modular: each engine solves one part of research."
- "It emphasizes transparent logic over black-box claims."

## How to Explain Safety Boundaries

- Research-only outputs
- No broker APIs
- No live trading
- No auto execution
- Human review always required

## How to Explain Future Roadmap

- Better engine test coverage and quality gates
- Deeper factor and scenario attribution
- More robust cross-asset and portfolio analytics
- Better UX polish and reporting exports
